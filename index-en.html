Worry-free renting

Your property works. We take care of the rest.

We work with owners who want to turn an empty, inherited or underused property into stable passive income without daily management.

We sign long-term contracts and pay rent every month, whether the rooms are occupied or not. We improve bathrooms, kitchens, flooring, electrics and plumbing at no cost to you, so the property is ready to attract good tenants.

Tenants receive free cleaning, wifi, utilities and a fully furnished room with its own lock, so they do not have to worry about anything.

Our profit comes from the margin created by the volume of properties and the scalability of our operation, not from charging you for each viewing, repair or incident.

Frequently asked questions

Questions owners often ask

Clear answers about managing your property and our room-rental model.

Does the owner have to pay anything?

No. Our operation covers preparation, viewings and management. We earn from the room-rental margin and economies of scale.

What happens if a room is empty?

Your rent remains as agreed in the contract. We take on the operation and occupancy risk.

Who handles repairs?

We coordinate and cover the ordinary repairs needed to keep the property in good condition.

What type of properties do you manage?

We assess each property according to its location, condition and demand, mainly in Madrid and nearby areas.

What do tenants receive?

Furnished rooms with locks, wifi, utilities and free cleaning, supported by active day-to-day management.

Can I request a valuation?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp and we will assess the property without obligation.
